# Quarterly Update

The second quarter brought steady progress across every team. Sales grew in each region, and the support desk answered most questions within a day.

## Highlights

Our new warehouse opened in April and now ships orders the same afternoon they arrive. Customers noticed the change, and returns fell by a fifth.

The design group finished the spring catalogue two weeks early, which left time to photograph the autumn range before the summer break.

## Next Steps

In the third quarter we will hire two engineers, move the billing system to the new servers and open a small office near the harbour.
