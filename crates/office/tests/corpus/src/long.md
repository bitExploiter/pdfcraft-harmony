# Rivers

## Part 1

Rivers shape the land slowly. Over thousands of years they carve valleys, carry sand to the sea and build wide plains where farms can grow.

A river begins as rain or melting snow high in the hills. Small streams join into larger ones, and the water gathers speed as it falls.

Where the slope eases, the river slows and drops the stones it carried. Gravel bars form, and the channel begins to wander from side to side.

Near the coast the water spreads into a delta. Fine mud settles there, and reeds and birds make it one of the richest places on the map.
